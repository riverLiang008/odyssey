# 01 / Motivation

> 可选章节卡：TXT-001

**▶ 镜头 005｜【R】真人正面开场**  `素材：R-001`

Recently, I started designing my own website.

**▶ 镜头 010｜【R+M】真人出镜；可穿插现有作品画面，不必制作新动画**  `素材：R-002 + EDIT-001`

I studied software engineering, and I have worked in this field. But I also have other interests. I write essays. I explore questions and turn my ideas into articles and models. I make videos about books, films, and my own thoughts. Recently, I also designed and built an AI Agent project called Odyssey.

**▶ 镜头 015｜【M｜蒙太奇】Odyssey、River、文章和视频素材依次出现，最后汇聚到网站**  `素材：EDIT-002 + SCR-001 + SCR-002 + GFX-001`

In the past, these things were separate. Software engineering was my work. Writing was a hobby. Videos were another way to express myself. They did not seem closely connected. But I realized they were all parts of the same person.

**▶ 镜头 020｜【R】真人点明网站目标**  `素材：R-003`

So I wanted one website to bring them together. It could be my portfolio and help me find a job. But I also wanted people to see more of who I am. So I started designing.


# 02 / First Portfolio

> 可选章节卡：TXT-002

**▶ 镜头 025｜【R】真人介绍 ChatGPT + Figma 的协作方式**  `素材：R-004`

I used ChatGPT and Figma. At first, my request was simple: minimal, calm, and well designed.

**▶ 镜头 030｜【M｜Figma】展示 02 整页，再聚焦四张作品卡片**  `素材：FIG-001`

That gave us version 02. It had a common portfolio layout: four project cards at the top, and my skills and experience below. There was nothing clearly wrong with it.

**▶ 镜头 035｜【R】真人解释求职目标和信息层级问题**  `素材：R-005`

But I soon saw a problem. The four cards might confuse someone visiting for the first time. This was not just a creative portfolio. I also wanted to use it to find work. A recruiter might first want to know: what can I do? What have I done? And what work shows these skills?

**▶ 镜头 040｜【M｜Figma】02→05 前后对比，强调卡片缩小和六个 Expertise 区域**  `素材：FIG-002`

So we changed the structure. In version 05, the four cards became smaller. Below them, six sections showed my skills, experience, and related work. The information was much clearer. I liked this version. But there was another problem.


# 03 / Too Much Like a Resume

> 可选章节卡：TXT-003

**▶ 镜头 045｜【M｜Figma】05→06 同构图对比，突出配色变化**  `素材：FIG-003`

The first colors felt too dark. The page looked consistent, but a little dull. So I asked ChatGPT to try new colors. That became version 06. It had a lighter background, dark text, and blue accents. It looked cleaner and was easier to read.

**▶ 镜头 050｜【R】真人表达第一次审美上的不满足**  `素材：R-006`

But after looking at it, something still felt wrong. It looked a bit too much like a nice resume. That is not bad. If I only wanted to show my skills, it might be enough. But I wanted the site to feel a little more like me. Not dramatic. Just less like a standard personal page. Then I had a new idea.


# 04 / Split the Page

> 可选章节卡：TXT-004

**▶ 镜头 055｜【R】真人说出原始设计构想**  `素材：R-007`

I asked ChatGPT: what if we split the page in two? Dark on the left, light on the right. Even one line of text could cross the split, and change color at the line.

**▶ 镜头 060｜【M｜新 Motion】简洁分屏动效，文字跨越分界线并反色；可复用 Figma 视觉**  `素材：M-01`

ChatGPT made version 07. At first, I liked it. The page now had a stronger visual identity.

**▶ 镜头 065｜【M｜Figma】07 整页慢速下滚，呈现重复分割和阅读压力**  `素材：FIG-004`

But it did not feel as balanced as I expected. The dark side felt too heavy. And the split continued all the way down the page. Maybe the colors were the problem.


# 05 / Maybe Not the Color

> 可选章节卡：TXT-005

**▶ 镜头 070｜【M｜Figma】08、09、10 三版本依次切换，可最后三栏并列**  `素材：FIG-005`

So we tried three more color sets. Version 08 used slate gray and warm white. Version 09 used sage green and ivory. Version 10 used blue-gray and cream. Each one felt different.

**▶ 镜头 075｜【R】关键转折；建议停顿**  `素材：R-008`

But none of them really fixed it. The page still felt wrong. Then I realized: maybe color was not the problem. Maybe the dark area was simply too large.

**▶ 镜头 080｜【R】解释布局问题，接到 11 的画面变化**  `素材：R-009`

At first, I used the split across the whole page. But maybe it did not need to be everywhere. The six skill sections had a lot of text. Making all that text cross the split again and again became distracting.

**▶ 镜头 085｜【M｜新 Motion】07→11：深色从整页收缩到 Hero；11 实际页面落版**  `素材：M-02 + FIG-006`

So we made the dark area smaller. We kept the split only at the top, and made the rest of the page light. That was version 11. Finally, it felt right. The top gave the page character. The lower part was easy to read. But I still wanted to change one small thing.


# 06 / 45 and 55

> 可选章节卡：TXT-006

**▶ 镜头 090｜【M｜新 Motion】11→12：分割线从 50:50 移动到 45:55，标出比例**  `素材：M-03 + FIG-007`

Version 11 used a perfect 50–50 split. It was balanced, but maybe too even. So we tried 45–55. That became version 12. It was a small change. We did not add anything new. But the layout felt more natural. I thought we were getting close. Still, I had one more idea.


# 07 / One More Idea

> 可选章节卡：TXT-007

**▶ 镜头 095｜【R】真人提出对角色块的构想**  `素材：R-010`

Looking at version 12, I had another idea. What if we added a dark block in the bottom-right corner, to match the one at the top-left? The two corners could echo each other.

**▶ 镜头 100｜【M｜Figma】12→16，突出右下角深色底及其中浅灰内容卡片**  `素材：FIG-008`

So we made version 16. It had some good points. The two dark corners made the page feel more complete. I also used lighter gray cards inside the bottom block, to match the project cards at the top. The design felt richer.

**▶ 镜头 105｜【R】真人提出关键取舍问题**  `素材：R-011`

But when I compared 12 and 16, I was not sure. Version 16 had more design. But did I really like it more?

**▶ 镜头 110｜【M｜Figma】12/16 并排对比，16 淡出，12 留下**  `素材：FIG-009 + EDIT-003`

The extra block made the layout look more complete, but also more forced. The skill sections were already clear and easy to read. The new dark area did not make them easier to understand. It mostly drew attention to the design itself. But I wanted people to see me and my work, not just the layout. So in the end, I chose version 12.


# 08 / Looking Back

> 可选章节卡：TXT-008

**▶ 镜头 115｜【R】真人回顾试错过程**  `素材：R-012`

Looking back, this was an interesting process. At first, I just wanted a simple, well-designed website. I did not know what the final version should look like. I kept trying ideas, looking at the results, and making changes.

**▶ 镜头 120｜【R+M】真人主画面，插入颜色实验、11/12/16 短蒙太奇**  `素材：R-013 + EDIT-004`

Sometimes I thought the colors were wrong. But the real problem was the layout. Sometimes I thought adding more design would help. But after trying it, I liked the simpler version more.

**▶ 镜头 125｜【R】真人总结 AI 协作与个人判断**  `素材：R-014`

ChatGPT helped me make many versions quickly. It also gave me useful advice. But it could not simply tell me which one was right for me. There was no single correct answer. I had to compare the versions and find out what I really wanted. That was the most interesting part.

**▶ 镜头 130｜【R】结尾观点，放慢语速**  `素材：R-015`

Design is not just about adding things. Sometimes, it means seeing that an idea does not work as well as you hoped, and being willing to remove it.

**▶ 镜头 135｜【M｜Figma】最终 12 全页，依次聚焦 Odyssey / Research / River / Films，最后静止**  `素材：FIG-010 + EDIT-005`

In the end, I got a website that was not too complex. It has some character, but it does not take attention away from the work. Software engineering, research, writing, videos, and all my projects: these are what I wanted to show. They may seem like different things. But they are all part of me. That is why I made this website.
