# Moonberry Island: Crops & Seasons

Guide ID: MOONBERRY-CROPS-01
Version: 1.0
Game ruleset: First Case demo, 2026-10-23

## Seasons

Each season lasts 28 days. Seasons repeat in this order: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter. Outdoor crops survive only in their listed season. Unharvested crops wither when the season changes. No winter crop is defined in this demo.

## Watering and harvest timing

Planting does not advance growth. Each overnight transition after a watered day adds one growth day; the morning after the required number of transitions, the crop is ready to harvest. Water on the planting day and on each subsequent day before maturity. Rain waters outdoor crops automatically. An unwatered day adds no growth.

A Turnip planted and watered on Spring Day 1 is ready on the morning of Spring Day 5 if every required day is watered. The latest planting day for a Turnip harvested within Spring is Day 24, yielding a Day 28 harvest under the same watering condition. Planting on Day 25 is too late. No fertilizer or greenhouse rules are included.
