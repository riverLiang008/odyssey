# Moonberry Island: Getting Started

Guide ID: MOONBERRY-START-01
Version: 1.0
Game ruleset: First Case demo, 2026-10-23

Moonberry Island is a fictional cozy farming game. These four guides define the demo rules; do not substitute rules from other games.

## Your first morning

You arrive on Spring Day 1 with 100 coins, a hoe, a watering can, and 5 Turnip Seeds. A fishing rod is not included. Visit Moss's Workshop to buy one; see the Village & Shops guide for its price and opening hours.

## Energy

Your maximum energy is 100. Tilling one tile costs 2 energy, watering one tile costs 1 energy, and casting a fishing line costs 3 energy. Walking, planting seeds, and visiting shops cost no energy. You cannot perform an action if you lack its energy cost.

## Rest and snacks

Sleeping restores energy to 100 and advances the date by one day. Eating a Moonberry restores 20 energy, up to the maximum of 100. This guide does not specify where Moonberries grow or their selling price.

## A small first-day plan

Till 5 tiles, plant your starter Turnip Seeds, and water all 5 tiles. Starting from full energy, tilling and watering these tiles costs 15 energy in total. For growth and harvest timing, see the Crops & Seasons guide.
