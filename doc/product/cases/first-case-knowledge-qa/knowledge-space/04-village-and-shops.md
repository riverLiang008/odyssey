# Moonberry Island: Village & Shops

Guide ID: MOONBERRY-SHOPS-01
Version: 1.0
Game ruleset: First Case demo, 2026-10-23

## Opening hours

Shops open every in-game day. Opening times are inclusive and closing times are exclusive. A shop listed as 09:00–18:00 accepts purchases at 09:00 but not at 18:00.

| Shop | Opening hours | What you can buy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pip's Seed Shop | 09:00–18:00 | Seeds for the current season |
| Moss's Workshop | 10:00–17:00 | Fishing rod for 50 coins |
| Nori's Snack Cart | 12:00–20:00 | Moonberry for 8 coins |

## Seeds

Pip sells Turnip Seeds in Spring for 10 coins each, Sunbean Seeds in Summer for 15 coins each, and Pumpkin Seeds in Autumn for 20 coins each. No winter seeds are defined in this demo. See the Crops & Seasons guide for growth and watering rules.

## Tools and snacks

A fishing rod is a one-time purchase in this demo; no tool upgrades or durability rules are defined. Moonberries restore energy as described in Getting Started. The guides do not expose a player's current wallet, inventory, weather, or time; provide those details in the question when needed.
