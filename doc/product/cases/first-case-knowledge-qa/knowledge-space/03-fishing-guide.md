# Moonberry Island: Fishing Guide

Guide ID: MOONBERRY-FISH-01
Version: 1.0
Game ruleset: First Case demo, 2026-10-23

## Before your first cast

Fishing requires a fishing rod. Buy one at Moss's Workshop; consult the Village & Shops guide for the price and opening hours. Each cast costs 3 energy. Matching a fish's conditions makes it available in the catch pool; it does not guarantee a catch.

## Fish reference

All times use the in-game clock. A time window includes its start and excludes its end: 16:00–20:00 means 16:00 is valid and 20:00 is not.

| Fish | Location | Season | Weather | Time window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Silverfin | Willow Pond | Spring or Autumn | Rainy only | 16:00–20:00 |
| Pebble Carp | Willow Pond | Any season | Any weather | 06:00–18:00 |
| Lantern Ray | Starfall Beach | Summer only | Clear only | 18:00–23:00 |

## Unknown details

Catch probabilities, bait bonuses, fish selling prices, and legendary fish are not specified by this demo. Do not assume a fish appears outside its listed conditions.
